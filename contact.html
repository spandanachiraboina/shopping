Frequently Asked

Quick answers to our most common questions.

What is your return policy? +
We offer hassle-free returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, with original tags attached. Simply visit your order history to initiate a return and receive a full refund or store credit.
How long does shipping take? +
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across India. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout. Orders over ₹2,999 qualify for free standard shipping automatically.
Do you offer international shipping? +
Yes! We ship to 40+ countries. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days. Customs and import duties may apply depending on your location.
How do I find my size? +
Each product page includes a detailed size guide with measurements in cm and inches. If you're between sizes, we recommend going up a size. Our team is also happy to provide personalised sizing advice.
Can I modify or cancel my order? +
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order enters our fulfilment process. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp for the fastest response.
Do you have a loyalty programme? +
Yes! Our Thread Rewards programme earns you points on every purchase, which can be redeemed for discounts. Sign up for a free account to start earning immediately.
